TextMemory

A memory for your browser

It saves the text of pages you read, on this computer, so you can find them again from any bit you remember.

  • It stays on this computer. No account, no server, no sync. Nothing you read is uploaded anywhere.
  • Only what you actually read. A page counts once it's been in front of you for a few seconds and you've scrolled it.
  • Some pages are skipped. Pages that ask for a password or card number, plus common sites in the categories you pick in a moment. You can add your own too.

What should it watch?

You can change this later in settings.

Skip these kinds of sites

Each category covers a lot of common sites, but not every one out there. You can add your own sites any time in settings.

How much should it keep?

Whichever limit is reached first applies. The oldest pages make room for new ones, every removal is listed in settings, and pinned pages are never removed.

What languages do you read?

If a word you search for finds nothing, search also tries its other forms in these languages. So running finds a page that says run.

Ticked based on your browser's languages. You can change this in settings.

Done

Press Ctrl Shift F to search, or tType tm then space in the address bar.
